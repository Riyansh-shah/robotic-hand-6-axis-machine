Results & Testing

Empirical validation through structured test prints — comparing dimensional accuracy, surface finish, and structural performance of the 6-axis arm against a baseline Cartesian FDM printer on identical geometries.

Positional Accuracy ±0.4mm RMS, post-compensation
Surface Quality Ra 7.2μm vs 28μm planar FDM
Support Reduction ~73% vs mandatory Cartesian supports
Tensile Strength Gain +47% non-planar vs standard FDM

Print #1 — Smooth Sphere

40mm diameter sphere printed with continuous spiral toolpath, nozzle always normal to surface. Ra = 7.2μm measured by profilometer. No visible layer lines. First validation of non-planar capability.

Material: PLA+ | Layers: continuous spiral | Time: 2h 14m

Print #2 — Tensile Test Bars

Dog-bone tensile specimens printed in both planar (vertical Z-layer) and non-planar (stress-aligned) configurations. Non-planar bars showed 47% higher ultimate tensile load in breaking tests, validating the strength improvement hypothesis.

Planar: 18.2 MPa | Non-planar: 26.8 MPa

Print #3 — Overhanging Bridge

70° overhang bridge geometry that requires extensive support with a Cartesian printer. Printed support-free by tilting the arm to keep the nozzle approach angle always within 35° of vertical relative to the print surface.

Support savings: 31g filament, 45min post-processing
