Chapter 02 — Physical System

Mechanical Design

A 6R fully-articulated arm configuration with mass-optimized links, stepper/servo actuation, and a direct-drive extruder end-effector. Every design decision is driven by the dual constraint of minimizing inertia while maximizing structural rigidity.

6R Articulated Configuration

The arm follows a 6-revolute (6R) serial chain topology — the most common industrial configuration (FANUC, KUKA, ABB all use this). All six joints are revolute (rotational), producing a robot with a complete 6-DOF workspace: three DOF for position (J1–J3) and three DOF for orientation (J4–J6).

The wrist is designed as a spherical wrist — axes J4, J5, and J6 all intersect at a single point (the wrist center). This critically decouples the position and orientation problems, enabling a clean analytical inverse kinematics solution.

The arm is split into two functional sub-assemblies:

  • J1–J3: Positioning assembly — places the wrist center at any point in the reachable workspace
  • J4–J6: Orientation assembly — spherical wrist controls nozzle approach direction

Structural Materials

Aluminium 6061-T6 — Base & Motor Mounts

Yield strength 276 MPa, density 2.7 g/cm³. CNC-machined base plate and motor mounts where dimensional tolerances and rigidity are critical. Anodized for corrosion resistance.

PLA+ / PETG — Link Bodies

Printed at 40% gyroid infill for the optimal stiffness-to-mass ratio. Link cross-sections are sized so first natural frequency stays above 20 Hz to avoid resonance during motion.

Steel M3/M4 Fasteners + Bearings

All joints use 608ZZ deep-groove ball bearings (8mm ID) for smooth rotation and minimal friction. Shoulder joint uses a 6804ZZ (20mm ID) for higher load capacity.

Actuator Selection

NEMA 17 Steppers — J1, J2, J3

1.8°/step (200 steps/rev), holding torque 0.59 N·m. Driven with TMC2208 drivers at 1/16 microstepping → 3200 steps/rev for smooth motion. Gear-reduced via cycloidal drive (ratio 21:1) for joint torque up to 12 N·m.

NEMA 14 Steppers — J4, J5

Smaller footprint for the wrist assembly. 0.40 N·m holding torque. Gear reduced 9:1 via bevel gear pair. Adequate for orientation control of the lightweight wrist.

MG996R Servo — J6 (Tool Roll)

High-torque metal gear servo, 11 kg·cm at 5V. Used for J6 continuous rotation with positional feedback via potentiometer. Easily interchangeable for future closed-loop upgrade.

End-Effector — Extruder Mount

The end-effector is a custom-designed direct-drive extruder mount that attaches to the J6 tool flange. The total end-effector assembly mass is kept under 220g — including motor, hotend, and part cooling fan — to stay within the arm's rated payload capacity.

The mount uses a 3-point kinematic coupling interface with M3 captive nuts, allowing the extruder to be swapped within 90 seconds without tools. This is important for maintenance and future multi-material upgrades.

A strain-relief bracket manages the six cable runs (heater, thermistor, part cooling fan, stepper motor, limit switch) that must flex through the full ±120° range of J5 without fatigue failure over thousands of print hours.
